  their own resource group and environment, deleted afterwards. Never touch
  `vlite-app`, `vlite-env`, `vlite-data`, `asendme-rg` or production storage.

  the existing Blob coordination store, the existing harness and examples.
- Results are evidence for proposals (OD-005 S_ctl, OD-006 replicas), not
  SLOs or qualification; `writer_only` routing stays the default.
- Dashboard support and EPIC-010 stay deferred.
