## Required tests

Deterministic: fake store, `std::sync::Barrier`/channels or `hook` for
interleavings, no sleeps, no wall-clock assertions. Name each test after the
property; prefix `fencing_fault_`.

1. TEST-007 double writer: two claims race on the same prepared record (use
   `hook` to run the second `claim` just before the first PUT). Exactly one
   wins; the loser gets `Lost`; only the winner can `advance_head`; the
   loser's `WriterFence` latches `Fenced` on its next `verify_before_commit`
   and `exit_code()` is `Some(1)`.
2. TEST-007 former owner after transfer: writer A serving, operator
   `prepare_transfer`, writer B claims. A's `advance_head` fails (`Fenced` or
   `HeadMoved`, never success); the record's head is unchanged by A; A's fence
   latches and stays latched even if the authority later becomes readable or a
   `transition(Serving)` is attempted.
3. TEST-007 head CAS 412: A reads, a competing PUT changes the ETag (hook),
   A's `advance_head` returns a typed CAS failure, never retries with the new
   ETag (count PUTs on the key via a wrapper or by inspecting `raw` before and
   after).
4. TEST-009 Unknown outcomes on `advance_head`, `claim`, `prepare_transfer`:
   `Fault::Before` (not applied) and `Fault::After` (applied) each resolve by
   exact readback; an unreadable readback (`Fault::After` PUT plus a refused
   GET) yields `Unresolved`, never success.
5. TEST-009 throttling/refusal: `Fault::Refuse` on GET makes
   `verify_before_commit` 503 without mutation and moves the fence to
   `AuthorityUnavailable`; recovery to `Serving` requires a fresh successful
   check; a refusal after a local commit (`verify_before_ack`) is 502
   `write_outcome_unknown` and the fence can never resume serving.
6. TEST-014 pause/zombie: writer A starts a check (`verify_before_ack`) whose
   GET is held by a hook until B has bumped the epoch and claimed; A's check
   then returns 502 and A is `Fenced`. Also: a check that began before a
   concurrent fence cannot clear it (generation rule).
7. TEST-014 skew: `claimed_unix_ms` far in the future or past (set via
   `change`/`set_raw` with a valid record) does not affect `verify_writer`
   or fence decisions — fencing is by epoch and writer id only.
8. TEST-008 loss bound: after a transfer, the frozen `base` equals A's last
   successful head exactly; a head A attempted after the transfer is never
   visible in the record.
9. Regression: authority object removed or replaced by garbage → `Missing` /
   `Invalid` latch `Fenced` with no further store writes from the fence.
