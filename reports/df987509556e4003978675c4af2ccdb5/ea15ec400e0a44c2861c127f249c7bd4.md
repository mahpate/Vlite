## Metrics, isolation and validity

### Disk and transient peaks

Measure logical bytes and allocated bytes per role: active reader; old reader; downloaded baseline; serialized and decoded tail; staging/partial files; index/sidecars; compaction input/output/scratch; logs; total filesystem occupancy. Include phase-start/end checkpoints and asynchronous polling through verification, swap and cleanup. Retain the maximum simultaneous total, not a sum of peaks that occurred at unrelated times.

**EXISTING REPO FACT:** allocated-byte implementation uses `st_blocks * 512`, counts directories recursively and rejects symlinks (`benchmarks/ha_reader_resource.rs:170-193`); the unit **512 B** is not a selected filesystem block size. Extend collection with filesystem free-space/quota telemetry and accounting for open-but-unlinked files; directory walks alone miss these and copy-on-write/shared-block accounting can differ. Record scan duration and accounting discrepancies. Keep source writer/fixture/model/storage outside the measured reader allocation; current resource phases include baseline plus case directories (`benchmarks/ha_reader_resource.rs:325-351`), so current totals must not be relabeled reader-only.

Sample transient disk peaks, including cleanup failures, interrupted staging and compaction coexistence. Use operation hooks/allocation accounting where available to bound shorter-lived allocations; sampling alone is a lower observation, not a guaranteed maximum. Preserve both the raw peak and its uncertainty; do not manufacture a corrected peak from a multiplier.

### Memory isolation

Collect process RSS and VmHWM plus cgroup memory current/peak/events, file/anonymous memory, swap and pressure. **EXISTING REPO FACT:** existing VmHWM is process-lifetime telemetry and includes model/harness/index state (`benchmarks/ha_reader_resource.rs:386-389`); it is not phase-local reader memory.

Proposed future harness method:

- Pre-generate immutable fixture, tail and expected-data hashes in a separate process outside the measured reader cgroup. Exit it before measurement; drive queries/validation from a separate controller.
- Launch a fresh reader child and fresh cgroup per strategy/replicate. Attribute only the reader child and its runtime descendants to reader memory. Run phases for startup, loaded-without-ANN, apply, detached full rebuild, index-ready serving, re-seed overlap and compaction. Record process and cgroup high-water independently.
- Measure index-associated memory with paired fresh-process ANN-off/on runs on identical data and staged before/after `smaps_rollup`/RSS snapshots. Differences are diagnostic estimates, not an exact allocator partition and never subtraction of unrelated lifetime peaks.
- Record absolute peaks for the actual simultaneously resident design, especially old reader/index plus replacement/replay/index. Do not subtract fixture memory from today's mixed-process peak and call the remainder isolated evidence.
- Put collector and load generator outside the reader cgroup, but record their host demand. Record page-cache effects in the reader cgroup; RSS alone does not determine container OOM risk.

### Sampling and repetitions

**SELECTED INPUT:** request **10 ms** polling, matching **EXISTING REPO FACT** `RESOURCE_SAMPLE_INTERVAL` (`benchmarks/ha_reader_resource.rs:38`). Retain each monotonic timestamp, phase identifier, scan duration, sample count, maximum gap and boundary samples. Existing output aggregates stats rather than retaining the full timeline (`benchmarks/ha_reader_resource.rs:65-77`); timeline retention is a gap.

**SELECTED INPUT:** a gap above **20 ms** or a scan duration above **10 ms** invalidates that run for peak qualification. Keep its artifacts labeled invalid; rerun the entire cell after correcting collector load, not just the missing window. This gate does not prove that shorter peaks were captured. Sampling even within budget requires complementary phase/allocation accounting or an explicitly approved uncertainty reserve (**OWNER DECISION**); absence of both blocks a hard bound.

**SELECTED INPUT:** perform **5 valid independent repetitions per cell**, with **1 separately labeled warmup** excluded from summaries; rotate lifecycle order, use fresh reader processes/fixtures and preserve failed/invalid attempts. **EXISTING REPO FACT:** old measurement mode also uses **5 repetitions** (`benchmarks/ha_reader_strategies.rs:37`), but resource mode does not implement this repetition protocol.

**SELECTED INPUT:** require `(maximum - minimum) / median <= 10%` across valid repetitions for allocated peak, reader process peak and cgroup peak separately. Retain min/median/max, raw repetitions and timing spread. If unstable, investigate and repeat rather than average away risk; unresolved spread leaves the cell unqualified. Stable repetitions are necessary, not proof of coverage or a statistical upper bound. **OWNER DECISION:** approve these sampling/variance settings or replace them before a campaign, without adjusting them retrospectively to pass results.

### Background-noise and failure policy

**SELECTED INPUT:** a **60 s** quiet preflight, with unrelated host CPU below **5%** and no unrelated I/O job on the measured storage. The host must also have exclusive scheduling for the run; low average usage on an otherwise shared machine is insufficient. Collect per-process activity and host pressure during runs. Unexpected unrelated CPU/I/O work, changing limits, unrelated cache churn or missing monitoring invalidates the run; retain it and rerun, not trim intervals.

Intentional CPU quota throttling, target pressure, OOM or ENOSPC induced by the workload are outcomes, not "noise" to discard. Correctness failure, hash mismatch, failed cleanup, missing telemetry, hidden retries, unsupported ANN behavior, an unverified applied position or sampling failure prevents a passing cell. Never convert failure to zero usage. Distinguish invalid instrumentation/host attempts from valid workload failures; both remain in the attempt ledger.

**EXISTING REPO FACT:** resource observer propagates sampling failures (`benchmarks/ha_reader_resource.rs:121-166`); the older logical-size compaction polling helper ignores scan errors (`benchmarks/ha_reader_strategies.rs:161-185`). The older helper alone cannot satisfy this validity policy.

## Deriving a ceiling and refusal contract

Do not set a ceiling from this report, the largest passing sample alone, advertised ephemeral capacity, or a multiplier on historical fixture results.

For each reviewed host/profile and allowed workload envelope, define:

- `D_evidence`: worst valid simultaneous allocated-disk peak across allowed lifecycle/overlap cells, with role accounting and sampling uncertainty.
- `M_evidence`: worst valid total reader-cgroup memory peak, retaining process/index breakdowns as diagnostics.
- `D_reserve`, `M_reserve`, uncertainty allowance and growth/backlog allowance: **OWNER DECISION**, explicitly justified; no numeric margin is proposed as an observed bound.
- Require projected peak plus reserves to fit actual usable tier storage/memory and current available resources. For a populated reader, compare additional required allocation against current free space; do not compare a total peak against free space without subtracting already-accounted occupancy.

The candidate logical-size ceiling is the largest **actually tested and reviewed** database-size band for which every required supported combination passes correctness, resource and stability gates with the approved reserves. Do not interpolate into untested bands or extrapolate to another tier. If resource use is not monotone, retain explicit admissible bands/conditions or choose a more conservative contiguous envelope; do not assume all smaller databases with different dimensions/history fit. Refusal must cover live records/dimensions/payload/history/tail/index/concurrency/scheduling constraints as well as bytes.

Define separate absolute enrollment ceiling and operational resource refusal threshold. Refuse enrollment if outside the qualified envelope or if projected peak plus approved reserves exceeds current resources. Recheck before each baseline/tail download, incremental apply, replacement and compaction because backlog, free space and other reservations change. Refuse or pause before crossing; never let a threshold merely warn after allocation. Boundary equality semantics, retryability and stale-read handling are **OWNER DECISION**; test exactly at, immediately below and immediately above each approved boundary using the protocol's native units.
