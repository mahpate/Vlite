Storage and records

Persistence and records
Single fileOne portable file, or fully in memory. Indexes can use sidecar files.
Durable commitsChecksummed and synced before a write succeeds.
Atomic batchesUp to 4,096 operations and 16 MiB, within one collection.
Conditional writesRevision checks. A conflict writes nothing.
CollectionsSeparate dimensions, IDs and settings in one file.
Record lifecycleExpiry, metadata-only updates and resumable imports.

Requirements and boundaries

  • The live dataset must fit in memory.
  • One writer, or several read-only readers, per file.
  • Some features need a newer file format. Upgrades are explicit.
  • No cross-collection transactions, distributed consensus or inner-product ANN.
  • Tenants share one file. Their isolation is logical, not cryptographic.
  • You supply the embeddings.
  • Durability depends on your filesystem and hardware.
