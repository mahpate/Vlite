ASENDME: share, create and play with nearby devices

Share, collaborate and play in your browser. No account or app install.

Open the app ↗ How it works ↗

Features

Share something. Make something. Play something.

Files, photos & links

Send files or a text/link card to a discovered device, individually or as a ZIP built on your device. Every recipient approves each offer.

Temporary Rooms

Bring up to 16 devices together with a code, link or QR — no GPS needed. Rooms expire after 30 minutes.

Conversations & ideas

Live nearby chat, shared notes, a shared whiteboard, voice notes and view-only screen sharing.

Everyday browser tools

Convert, format, generate and inspect data — JSON, Base64, hashes, tokens, units, dates, colours and images — plus a personal Excalidraw canvas.

Solo, co-op & versus games

An arcade of quick solo games, cooperative puzzles and head-to-head classics like Chess, Backgammon and Draw & Guess.

Your devices, connected

Pair with a one-time code or QR and compare the PIN and emoji on both screens. Optionally remember up to 8 of your own devices.

Get started

From hello to sent in three steps

Step 1

Open ASENDME on both devices

Pick a display name. Use optional location discovery, or skip it and join a Room or pair by code.

Step 2

Find the right device

Create a Room and share its code or QR, pair a device, or pick a visible nearby device.

Step 3

Choose an activity

Send a file, link or note, start a whiteboard or invite someone to a game. They choose whether to accept.

How your content travels

Direct when possible, encrypted always

ASENDME does not store your shared files, chat, notes or drawings on a server.

01

A server introduces devices

A signaling service handles discovery, invitations and connection details. You choose who to connect with.

02

Direct P2P when possible

After acceptance, browsers try an encrypted direct connection for the content.

03

Encrypted relay if needed

Supported activities can fall back to a bounded encrypted relay. “Direct only” disables it; screen sharing is never relayed.

Choose how to connect

Nearby, across the room, or by code

Nearby discovery

Orbit is a visual radar and Nearby a grouped list of devices around you, with an optional map. Optional same-network discovery groups opted-in devices that share a public IP.

Rooms & pairing without GPS

Share a Room code, link or QR privately, or pair with a single-use code that expires in 10 minutes. Room polls and game scoreboards are built in.

More control

Transfers and privacy, on your terms

Send queue

Pause, reorder and choose 1–3 simultaneous outgoing sends.

Integrity checks

Opt-in SHA-256 comparison for eligible files up to 8 MiB.

Media previews

Preview accepted files and save selections or a ZIP from the session gallery.

Visibility & Ghost

Stay visible, go visible for ten minutes, or go Ghost to stop discovery and new requests.

Works across Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iOS and ChromeOS in current browsers, and installs as a progressive web app. Feature support varies by browser.

Ready when you are

Open on both devices. Free, no account required.
