## Required behaviour (content of `docs/HA-ACA.md`)

Plain-language, task-first, at most ~2500 words. It must state clearly, near
the top:
1. HA is opt-in. With none of the HA flags or files, VLite runs exactly as the
   single-writer deployment does today (no new headers, fields, reads,
   errors or startup requirements).
2. Gateway and reader scale-out add read capacity and isolation; they are not
   writer HA. There is one writer.
3. The writer serving fence is not wired into the shipped binary yet, and
   there is no automatic failover. Failover is an operator-run, reported and
   acknowledged procedure.
4. Azure Files remains experimental and is not power-loss qualified.

Runbooks (each with: when to use it, prerequisites, exact commands, how to
verify, how to back out):
5. Planned and unplanned writer failover with `vlite-server failover`
   `report` then `execute` (old writer scaled to zero replicas and confirmed
   stopped first; the exact report acknowledgement; what to check after).
6. Replacing or adding a reader with `--serve-mode reader
   --reader-apply-file` (config fields and limits, the served path rule,
   readiness, the `x-vlite-*` position/age headers, `reader_stale` and
   `reader_apply_failed`).
7. Control-state outage and refresh with `vlite-server control-state
   publish|update` (what fails closed, how gateways and trusted backends
   recover).
8. Gateway signing-key rotation with `vlite-server gateway-keygen` and the
   staged overlap procedure (link to `deploy/azure-container-apps/ha/README.md`
   "Manual staged overlap rotation (not automated)").
9. Restore to a log-archive checkpoint with `vlite-server cloud-sets` and
   `cloud-restore --archive-checkpoint latest|<sequence>` (rekey only; seed
   mode is refused).
10. Secrets: use the existing Container Apps secrets the same way as the admin
    token (no Key Vault or other new service). Never put keys or tokens in
    command lines, logs or the docs' examples (use placeholders and files).
11. A short "What is not provided" list (automatic failover, multi-writer,
    cross-region, power-loss qualified Azure Files, query routing of writes
    to readers).
